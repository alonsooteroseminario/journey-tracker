# Prompt Wallet: Instagram Launch Plan

Campaign for the free public beta of Prompt Wallet on `@buildcadence.co`.
Primary goal: **new followers.** Secondary: beta signups and real feedback.

Reads with `about-me.md`, `voice.md`, `reference-teardown.md`.

## The strategic call

The account is Build Cadence. The product on offer is Prompt Wallet. The other
Cadence features stay "coming soon" until they ship publicly.

**Positioning: the prompt organization account.** Own one narrow idea so
completely that anyone thinking about managing prompts thinks of this account.

**The one rule, from the teardown:** the 65k reel ran 18:1 you/your to I/me.
The 4k reel ran 0:9. Same niche, same production quality, 15x the reach. So
every asset teaches the viewer about their world. The product is the
demonstration, never the subject. "Look what I built" is banned from hooks.

**Central mental model:** *Stop writing prompts. Start assembling them.*
That line is the campaign's portable takeaway, the equivalent of the outlier's
"brain, library, librarian".

## Profile rewrite

The current bio sells streaks and daily goals. A person who signs up today gets
redirected to `/wallet` and sees none of that. Fix before anything ships.

## Content pillars

1. **Prompt craft**: writing and structuring a prompt worth keeping
2. **Prompt organization**: systems for keeping what works
3. **AI workflows**: how the work actually gets done, day to day

## Format stack

Three posts a week. Reels carry reach, which is the follower goal, and Remotion
makes them cheap for this account specifically.

| Day | Format | Length | Job | Built with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Explainer reel | 60 to 150s | reach | Remotion |
| Wed | Carousel, 6 slides, 4:5 | n/a | saves | `next/og` |
| Fri | Short reel | 20 to 40s | reach, volume | Remotion + screen capture |

**Explainer reel spec** (from the outlier): numbered chapter tags top-left,
~5s average shot, voiceover plus low ambient music, subtle UI clicks on
transitions, stylized UI in floating rounded cards, never raw fullscreen
capture. Close on the mental model. A closing CTA is fine when it names the
artifact the viewer just received, never the product (teardown round 2).

**Carousel spec** (from theedgarr): slide 1 is a real photo with a huge
condensed headline in white. Slides 2 to 6 are flat cream ground, headline in
one accent color, product UI as floating dark rounded cards with drop shadows,
overlapping and slightly offset. Wordmark small and top center on every slide.

**Caption spec** (from `voice.md`): hook under 12 words, 2 to 4 short body
lines, one CTA that names a specific moment, exactly 4 hashtags on their own
line. Never a bare "save this". No em dashes.

## Launch arc

**Week 0, pre-launch.** Rewrite the profile. Bank the first 3 posts before
going live. An account that posts once and goes quiet reads as dead.

**Week 1, the problem.** No product. Establish that prompt loss is a real,
named thing. This is where followers are earned.

**Week 2, the method.** Assembling beats writing. Teach the chunk model as an
idea, independent of any tool.

**Week 3, the product.** Prompt Wallet as the demonstration of week 2.

**Week 4, proof and feedback.** Real usage, real questions, open the beta loop.

## What to measure

Follower growth is the goal, but it is a lagging number. The leading ones:

- **Watch time on reels.** The single strongest predictor of reach.
- **Saves per carousel.** Saves are why carousels exist here.
- **Follows per post**, in Instagram's per-post insights. Names the format that
  actually converts.
- **Profile visits to follows.** A poor ratio means the bio is the problem, not
  the content.

Reassess after 9 posts. Kill whichever of the three formats converts worst and
double the winner. Do not decide before 9.
