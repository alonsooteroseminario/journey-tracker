# Voice Profile

Derived from the 9 approved captions in `social-media-kit.md` (2026-09-03).
Those captions are all about goals and streaks, so what follows is **rhythm and
structure only**. The subject matter does not carry over to Prompt Wallet.
American English, no em dashes, per house style.

## Who I sound like

Someone who diagnoses before selling. It names a failure the reader recognizes,
explains the mechanism behind it in one or two flat sentences, then shows the
product as the fix for that mechanism. It never raises its voice and never
celebrates itself. Quietly certain, closer to a good postmortem than an ad.

## Tone

Plain, diagnostic, unsentimental, specific, quietly confident.

Never hits: hype (no "powerful", "amazing", "game-changing", "effortless"),
preachiness, or cheerleading. Nothing in 9 of 9 samples congratulates the reader
or exhorts them to believe in themselves.

## Sentence rhythm

Short. Most sentences run 4 to 12 words, none over about 20. Deliberate
fragments carry weight: "No blank page." "Motivation fades." Body copy is 2 to 4
lines with hard line breaks, not one flowing paragraph. Structure is fixed:
hook, blank line, body, blank line, one CTA, blank line, exactly 4 hashtags.

## Hook patterns

- **Contrarian correction.** "Most goals don't die in week 1. They die in week 3."
- **Two-beat aphorism.** "Motivation fades. A streak doesn't let you forget."
- **Negation stack.** "No blank page. No 40-minute planning session that becomes procrastination."
- **Imperative sequence.** "Set the goal. Get the daily tasks. Keep the streak."
- **Flat observation.** "Progress feels different when someone is watching."

Absent across all 9: rhetorical questions, "imagine if", "here's the thing",
"let that sink in". No caption opens with a question.

## How I open

First line lands in under 12 words and states something the reader already
suspects but has not put into words. It never introduces the product first.

## How I close

One CTA, always imperative, and **never bare**. The action is always attached to
a specific moment or person: "Save this and reread it in week 3." "Send this to
the friend with 9 half-started goals." A naked "save this" appears 0 of 9 times.
Never "link in bio."

## Signature phrases

The double-dot pause (`..`) where an em dash would go. The split correction
("Don't start from zero. Fork a plan that already worked."). The colon reveal
("That's the whole gap: no daily instruction, no visible cost to skipping").
Concrete counts used as nouns: "9 half-started goals", "week 3", "60 seconds".

## Off-limits

- Em dashes (0 of 9). Use `..`, a comma, or a line break.
- Exclamation marks (0 of 9).
- Rhetorical questions (0 of 9).
- ALL CAPS (0 of 9).
- Emoji beyond one functional mark per caption (1 of 9 used a single fire icon).
- Hashtags inside the body. They sit on their own final line, always 4.
- Corporate verbs: leverage, unlock, empower, streamline, supercharge.

## What this voice never does

- Opens with the product name. The problem always comes first.
- Uses an adjective where a number would work.
- Writes a CTA without naming the moment it applies to.
- Runs a sentence past roughly 20 words.
- Explains a benefit twice in one caption. Each line does new work.
